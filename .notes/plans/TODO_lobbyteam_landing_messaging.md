# LobbyTeam Landing Page Messaging

## Objective

Replace the current AI-receptionist-led story with an outcome-led landing page that speaks to the business problem, the target operating contexts, and LobbyTeam's intelligent human-routing outcome.

## Checklist

- [ ] Critique the current landing-page message and identify positioning risks.
- [ ] Define the approved outcome-led page narrative and persona entry points.
- [ ] Draft final homepage copy and conversion path.
- [ ] Review and approve the messaging before changing frontend components.
- [ ] Implement, test, and deploy the approved copy.

## Decisions

- Lead with the business outcome, not AI agents, receptionist replacement, or channel coverage.
- Position LobbyTeam as the business front door across office, phone, website, and distributed support contexts.
- Make intelligent routing to the right human or team a central differentiator.
- Keep “AI” as an explanation of how the outcome is delivered, not the primary promise.
- Do not change public site code until the messaging narrative is reviewed.
