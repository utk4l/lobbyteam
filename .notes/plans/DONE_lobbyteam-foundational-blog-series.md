## Editorial Decisions

- Write for owners and operations leaders first, with clear examples for office, online, appointment, and support contexts.
- Explain LobbyTeam after the problem and operating framework, not in the opening sentence.
- Use “front door” as the shared category language while clarifying that it spans phone, website, email, messaging, and configured physical-lobby channels.
- Do not promise that automation resolves every request; show where configured workflows complete work and where accountable humans own the decision.
