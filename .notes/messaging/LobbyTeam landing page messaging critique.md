# LobbyTeam Landing Page Messaging Critique

## Current Message

The current landing page leads with:

> **The AI Receptionist That Never Misses a Job**

and:

> **Every customer greeted. Every call answered. Without hiring.**

The page then explains Converso as an AI front desk that answers calls, works out what people need, books appointments, and follows up.

## What Is Working

- The page uses concrete customer situations rather than abstract software language.
- “Front desk” is a useful mental model for an office or appointment-driven business.
- The live conversation visual makes the product feel tangible.
- The page communicates that conversations become notes, actions, and follow-up.
- The human takeover and audit concepts create trust.
- The design already has a strong editorial and operational tone.

## What Is Weak Or Risky

### 1. The hero starts with the product category, not the buyer's problem

“AI receptionist” asks the buyer to understand the mechanism before they understand the benefit. The buyer is more likely thinking:

- We miss calls when everyone is busy.
- Requests arrive after hours and wait too long.
- The wrong person gets the request.
- Staff repeat the same information.
- Customers do not know what happens next.

The hero should begin with the business consequence and the result LobbyTeam creates.

### 2. “Without hiring” sends the wrong signal

LobbyTeam is not simply a labor replacement. It can route a customer to a human, including a person in another region or shift. “Without hiring” risks making the product sound like a cost-cutting receptionist substitute instead of an intelligent front door that helps the existing team work better.

### 3. “Every call answered” is too narrow

The product story includes website chat, email, SMS, WhatsApp, physical lobby entry points, outbound calls, after-hours routing, and distributed support teams. A phone-first headline makes the online-business and follow-the-sun use cases feel secondary.

### 4. “Every customer greeted” is pleasant but not decisive

Greeting is only the beginning. The differentiator is what happens after arrival:

- The request is understood.
- The right work is completed.
- The right person is found.
- Context follows the handoff.
- The customer does not have to start over.

### 5. “Converso” conflicts with the LobbyTeam direction

If LobbyTeam is the public product name, the page cannot continue to make Converso the dominant brand. A rename should happen after messaging approval, not before, but the final narrative should consistently use LobbyTeam.

### 6. The current persona story is too generic

“Small businesses that sell by phone and chat” is not enough. The page should let visitors recognize their situation:

- The office whose receptionist is handling one person while another call waits.
- The appointment business managing confirmations, reminders, and rescheduling.
- The online business with unanswered website, email, and chat inquiries.
- The service business dispatching the right technician.
- The global support team routing U.S. after-hours requests to India and daytime requests to the U.S. team.

## Recommended Positioning

### Core promise

> **Make sure every customer reaches the right next step.**

### Supporting statement

> **LobbyTeam is the front door for your business. It answers calls and messages, understands what people need, completes the work you define, and routes the rest to the right human with useful context.**

### Product explanation

> **It works across the office, phone, website, email, SMS, and WhatsApp, so your customers get one clear path forward even when your team, channels, and schedules are spread out.**

### Coordinated team explanation

This is a public differentiator, not secret sauce. Buyers need to understand why LobbyTeam is more than a single receptionist bot, but the explanation should follow the outcome rather than lead the hero.

> **Not just an AI receptionist. LobbyTeam is a coordinated digital front-door team. One agent engages the customer while specialized agents help interpret the request, apply your rules, and guide what happens next.**

Supporting outcome:

> **Your customer gets progress. Your team gets context. Someone always knows what should happen next.**

The site should say that the business defines the knowledge, workflows, routing rules, escalation rules, and approved actions. Avoid implying that agents make unrestricted decisions. Humans remain accountable for sensitive, technical, commercial, security, emergency, clinical, legal, and other judgment-heavy decisions.

### Human boundary

> **LobbyTeam does not replace your people. It makes sure the right request reaches the right person at the right time, with the conversation already understood.**

## Proposed Above-The-Fold Direction

### Eyebrow

**Your business front door, covered.**

### Headline options

1. **Make sure every customer reaches the right next step.**
2. **Your front door should move people forward.**
3. **Every arrival deserves a clear path forward.**
4. **Stop losing customers between hello and next step.**

Recommended headline:

> **Make sure every customer reaches the right next step.**

### Hero paragraph

> LobbyTeam answers calls and messages, understands what people need, handles the requests you define, and routes the rest to the right human. At your office, after hours, or through your website, customers get progress instead of voicemail, silence, or another handoff.

### Primary CTA

**See how LobbyTeam can cover your front door**

### Secondary CTA

**Start with your business context**

## Recommended Page Flow

### 1. Name the shared problem

> Customers do not care which channel they used or which department owns the answer. They expect one business to understand what they need and help them move forward.

Show the fragmented reality: phone, inbox, website, lobby, calendar, CRM, shifts, and on-call coverage.

### 2. Show the outcome

Four outcome cards:

- **Answered:** the customer gets a useful response.
- **Handled:** LobbyTeam completes the defined request.
- **Routed:** the right person or team receives the request.
- **Followed through:** the context and next action remain visible.

### 3. Let buyers recognize their context

Use four entry points instead of one generic “who it is for” section:

- **At the office:** support visitors, callers, appointments, and host connections.
- **On the phone:** answer, qualify, schedule, remind, reschedule, and route.
- **On the website:** respond to chat, email, forms, and inbound sales or support questions.
- **Across time zones:** route after-hours requests to the right regional or on-call human.

### 4. Make intelligent routing the proof of difference

Explain that routing can use:

- Time zone and business hours
- Availability and shift status
- Location and language
- Expertise and request type
- Urgency and service level
- Account or relationship ownership
- Primary and backup contacts

This makes LobbyTeam materially different from a voicemail replacement, chatbot, or simple call-forwarding rule.

### 5. Show one concrete story

> A U.S. customer calls after hours with a support issue. LobbyTeam understands the request, checks the routing rules, connects the customer to the available India-based support team, and preserves the context. During U.S. business hours, the same request routes to the U.S. team.

### 6. Explain how it works without leading with AI

Use a simple sequence:

**Arrive → understand → handle → route → follow through**

Then explain that specialized digital agents perform this work behind the scenes. The architecture is proof of delivery, not the headline.

### 7. Address the human relationship

Show that LobbyTeam works alongside:

- Receptionists
- Office managers
- Support teams
- Sales teams
- Dispatchers
- Regional and on-call specialists

The product reduces interruption and improves context. It does not remove human ownership where judgment, expertise, or security decisions are required.

### 8. Present pricing after the outcome is clear

Use the approved Interaction Unit model:

- `$49/month` for 20 units, `$0.99` overage
- `$99/month` for 50 units, `$0.79` overage
- `$299/month` for 500 units, `$0.59` overage

Price the outcome and explain the meter in plain language. Do not lead the page with “AI actions,” tokens, or channel-specific technical costs.

## Messages To Remove Or Reframe

- Replace “AI receptionist” with “business front door” or “front-door team.”
- Replace “without hiring” with “without leaving the next step to chance.”
- Replace “every call answered” with “every customer reaches a clear next step.”
- Replace “AI agent” as the headline with the customer result; explain agents later.
- Replace “website chatbot” with “website requests answered and moved forward.”
- Replace “routing” as a technical feature with “the right person gets the request with context.”

## Decisions Needed Before Copy Implementation

- Is LobbyTeam the final public brand replacing Converso everywhere?
- Which four contexts are launch priorities on the homepage?
- Is live phone transfer available now, or should the site say “request a human connection” until verified?
- Which appointment, CRM, calendar, email, SMS, WhatsApp, and routing capabilities are production-ready?
- Is the primary launch buyer a small-business owner, an office manager, or a support/operations leader?
- Should the homepage use one universal CTA or context-specific CTAs for office, online, and global support buyers?
