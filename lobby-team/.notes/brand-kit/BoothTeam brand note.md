# BoothTeam Brand Note

## Public product name

**BoothTeam by SophiMind**

Always write the product name as `BoothTeam` with a capital `B` and capital `T`.

## Descriptor

> **The digital booth team for trade shows.**

Use the descriptor near the first public mention so visitors understand that BoothTeam is digital support for exhibitors, not human booth staffing.

## Voice

Prefer personal, outcome-led language:

- **Your BoothTeam** helps every engaged visitor find the right next step.
- Train **your BoothTeam** around your company, products, visitors, and event goals.
- **Your BoothTeam** works alongside the humans you send to the show.

Avoid leading with “AI,” “agents,” or internal architecture. Explain those mechanics only when they help clarify how the outcome is delivered.

## Boundary

BoothTeam does not provide human booth employees. SophiMind provides the human setup and coaching that helps the exhibitor configure and guide BoothTeam.

## Transition

`AYS` and `At Your Service` may remain in internal code identifiers, legacy URLs, historical documents, and migration notes. They should not appear as the primary public product name after the rename.

## Example

> **BoothTeam by SophiMind** gives you a digital booth team trained for your next trade show, so every engaged visitor can find a relevant, actionable next step.
