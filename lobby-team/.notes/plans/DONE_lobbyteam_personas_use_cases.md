# LobbyTeam Personas And Use Cases

## Objective

Define the business contexts, buyer personas, operating personas, and customer stories that LobbyTeam should serve before rewriting the public website messaging.

## Checklist

- [x] Document the confirmed office and online-business contexts. DONE
- [x] Map missing use cases and rank them as core, adjacent, or later. DONE
- [x] Identify buyer, operator, and end-customer personas for the messaging framework. DONE
- [x] Review the working map with the product owner before changing public site copy. DONE

## Decisions

- “Office setting” means a brick-and-mortar business with a physical front desk or reception function.
- “Website” means an online business whose front door is website, email, chat, phone, SMS, or WhatsApp.
- LobbyTeam should be positioned around progress from first contact to an owned next step, not around a channel or a generic AI assistant.
- This phase is messaging discovery only; no public site copy is changed until the persona map is reviewed.
